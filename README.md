# Desktop Assistant ✨

Clippy for the AI age. A small, overly helpful assistant that lives on your Mac's desktop.
It wanders around, walks on top of your windows, sits on the Dock, and offers "help" nobody asked for.

## Run it

```bash
./build.sh
```

This builds the app, installs it in `/Applications` and restarts it. Use `./build.sh --no-install` to only build into `build/`.
Afterwards, start it like any other app (Spotlight: "Desktop Assistant"), or turn on **Launch at Login** in the ✨ menu.

Look for the ✨ in the menu bar. There's no Dock icon. During development, `swift run` also works from this folder.

The app icon is generated by `swift tools/generate-icon.swift Resources`.

## What it does

- **Lives above everything**: it floats over windows, the menu bar and the Dock, on every Space and over full-screen apps. Clicks on transparent areas go through to whatever is underneath.
- **Physics**: it walks around, jumps, falls with gravity, lands on the tops of other windows and rides along when you move them. When a window closes it falls.
- **Drag and throw**: pick it up and fling it. It has opinions about this.
- **Click it**: opens an "assistant" menu of AI features nobody asked for (Summarize My Screen, Make It More Engaging, Upgrade to Pro+ Ultra Max…).
- **Ask Me Anything** (click it, or use the ✨ menu): a chat box next to the creature. Under the hood it's an ELIZA-style pattern matcher (`ChatBrain.swift`) with real skills: math, unit conversion, definitions from the macOS dictionary, the time (also in other cities), reminders and timers, opening apps and websites, web search, coins, dice and jokes. Reminder bubbles stay up until you click them. Anything else gets a confidently wrong answer. Type `help` to see the list. Return sends, Esc closes, ↑ recalls earlier questions.
- **Rewrite Clipboard** (click it): copy text anywhere, pick a style, and paste the result. The styles are More Engaging, LinkedIn, Corporate, Pirate and Mocking, plus useful ones: UPPERCASE, lowercase, Title Case and Clean Up Spacing. Undo Last Rewrite restores the original. Also works from the chat: "rewrite as pirate", "make it corporate", "undo". The clipboard is only read when you ask (`TextRewriter.swift`).
- **Drop a file on it** (or choose **Summarize a File…** from its menu) for an "AI summary" that's really just honest stats with a punchline. It handles text, code, PDFs, images, video and audio, folders, apps and multiple files at once. Everything runs locally with built-in frameworks (`FileSummarizer.swift`).
- **Context-aware heckling**: it reacts to the app you switch to, the time of day, long typing sessions and low battery.
- **Streams its answers** word by word, sometimes after a fake "Reasoning…" pause.
- **Petting and mood**: wiggle the cursor back and forth over it and it purrs and shows hearts. Its mood (shown in the ✨ menu) rises when you're nice to it and falls when you're not. Happy assistants play more; sad ones mope.
- **Playing**: throw it and it runs back to where you threw it from, wanting another go (fetch). It sometimes chases your cursor (or click it and choose **Let's Play!**, or type "chase me"). It also peeks in from screen edges, climbs the sides of windows and dangles from window edges.
- **The Trash**: while you drag it, a trash can appears in the corner. Drop it in and it's deleted, dramatically, then comes back from "the cloud". **Use Real Dock Trash** in the ✨ menu targets the Dock's Trash instead (needs Accessibility permission).
- **Interns**: ignore it long enough and it hires a smaller helper. Interacting with it sends the interns home; clicking one pops it.
- **Costumes**: a pumpkin in October, a Santa hat in December, a party hat on New Year's Day, or pick one manually under **Costume**. Sprite characters set where the hat goes with `"hat"` in `character.json`.
- **Effects**: glitter trail when thrown, plus confetti and hearts (`Effects.swift`, GPU particle emitters in click-through overlay windows).
- **Settings window** (✨ menu → **Settings…**, ⌘,): character, movement, costume, size and speed sliders, chattiness, feature toggles, quiet mode, quiet hours and launch at login (`AppSettings.swift`, `SettingsView.swift`).
- **Quiet mode**: during calls (Zoom, Teams, FaceTime, Webex) and whenever an app is full screen (presentations, videos) it hides, or just stays silent, depending on the setting. Optional quiet hours.
- **Clippy buttons**: some questions come with **[Yes] [No] [Don't show again]**. "Don't show again" works. Eventually.
- **Daily report** (click it → **My Daily Report**, or the ✨ menu): a fake-"AI" report built from real numbers: active time, keystroke count (never which keys), time per app, app switches, and how you treated it. In the evening it offers to show it. Seven days of history are kept (`DayStats.swift`).
- **Sticky notes**: occasionally it leaves a little note on your screen; click to crumple it. Turn it off in Settings (`StickyNotes.swift`).
- **Day and night**: a nightcap late at night (when it's also sleepier and slower), a coffee mug in the morning.
- **Feelings**: dismiss two bubbles in a row and it sulks. Leave the Mac alone for a while and it falls asleep, then wakes up when you're back.
- **Annoyance Level** (menu bar): Polite → Eager → Clingy → Unhinged.
- **Snooze for 1 Hour** when you need some peace.

It needs no permissions. Window *positions* are readable without Screen Recording access, and it never reads window contents or titles. Nothing leaves your Mac.

## Characters

Looks are separate from behavior. Switch them from the ✨ menu (**Character**) or by clicking the creature (**Change My Look**).

| Character | How it's made |
|---|---|
| **Sparkle** | Drawn in code (`Sources/DesktopAssistant/Characters/SparkleCharacter.swift`): a googly-eyed ✨ that follows your cursor with its eyes |
| **Cookie Banner** | Drawn in code (`CookieBannerCharacter.swift`): a "We use cookies" banner on little legs, with Reject/Accept buttons that react to its mood |
| **Ghost** | Drawn in code (`GhostCharacter.swift`): a slightly see-through ghost with a waving hem that floats instead of walking |
| **Rubber Duck** | Pixel art (`Resources/Characters/rubberduck/`, generated by `tools/generate-duck.swift`). In the chat it does real rubber-duck debugging and answers with questions that help you explain your bug |
| **Mr. Byte** | Frame-by-frame pixel art (`Resources/Characters/mrbyte/`), a retro beige computer with legs. The frames are generated by `tools/generate-mrbyte.swift` |

### Add your own

Choose **Open Characters Folder…** from the ✨ menu (`~/Library/Application Support/DesktopAssistant/Characters`).
Create a folder containing PNG frames and a `character.json`, for example:

```json
{
  "name": "My Character",
  "frameWidth": 32, "frameHeight": 32, "scale": 3,
  "footInset": 1,
  "walkSpeed": 60, "bounciness": 0.3,
  "facesRight": true,
  "movement": "walk",
  "hat":  { "x": 16, "y": 6, "width": 12 },
  "hand": { "x": 28, "y": 22, "height": 8 },
  "animations": {
    "idle": { "frames": ["idle_0.png", "idle_1.png"], "fps": 2 },
    "walk": { "frames": ["walk_0.png", "walk_1.png"], "fps": 8 }
  },
  "quips": ["Something only this character would say."]
}
```

Only `idle` is required. The other states are `walk`, `fall`, `drag`, `talk`, `sulk`, `sleep` and `happy`, and any that are missing reuse `idle`.
`hat` and `hand` (both optional, in source pixels from the top-left) say where hats sit and where held props like the morning coffee go.
Then choose **Reload Characters**. `Resources/Characters/mrbyte/` is a complete example.

For a look drawn in code (vector, Lottie, 3D…), implement `CharacterPack` and a `CharacterView` subclass and register it in `CharacterLibrary.loadAll()`.
Subclass `GooglyCharacterView` instead to get the googly face, poses and extras of the built-in characters and only draw a body.
To keep a code-drawn character out of the repository (fan art, say), put it in `Sources/DesktopAssistant/Characters/Local/`, which git ignores, and hand it over through a `LocalCharacters` class there (see `LocalCharacterSource` in `CharacterLibrary.swift`).

### Movement styles

Each character has its own gait: **walk** (Mr. Byte, Cookie Banner), **waddle** (Rubber Duck), **hop** (Sparkle) or **float** (Ghost). There's also **roll**.
Floaters hover above the ground and drift down slowly; hoppers really jump; rollers turn like a wheel and stand back up when they stop.
Override it for any character under **Movement** in the ✨ menu. Sprite characters set theirs with `"movement"` in `character.json`.

## Troubleshooting

The ✨ menu shows a **Status** line describing what the assistant is doing (napping, sulking, climbing…).
For more detail, run it from a terminal with `DA_DEBUG=1 build/DesktopAssistant.app/Contents/MacOS/DesktopAssistant`, which prints its internal state every second.

## Code map

Everything is in `Sources/DesktopAssistant/`:

| Folder | What's in it |
|---|---|
| `App/` | Entry point, app delegate and the menu bar icon |
| `Assistant/` | The brain: when to talk and what to say. `Assistant.swift` has the update loops, presence (snoozing, quiet mode) and settings; the `Assistant+…` files have talking, reactions to events, actions, interns and the menus |
| `Creature/` | The body: its window and frame update (`Creature.swift`), walking, falling and multiple screens (`+Movement`), climbing, hanging, peeking and the other activities (`+Activities`), petting, shaking and throwing (`Gestures.swift`), and `World.swift`: what it can stand on |
| `Characters/` | The looks: `CharacterPack` and `CharacterView`, the shared googly face (`GooglyCharacterView.swift`), the four drawn characters, sprite characters, and hats and props (`Accessories.swift`) |
| `Models/` | Settings, mood, daily stats and report insights, annoyance levels, and everything it says (`Quips.swift`) |
| `Services/` | The chat brain and calculator, text rewrites, file summaries, reminders, the clipboard, quiet-mode detection, the Dock's Trash and system info |
| `Views/` | The overlay windows, speech bubble, chat box, sticky notes, effects and trash can, and the SwiftUI Settings and Daily Report windows |
| `Support/` | Small helpers: colors, screen geometry, shapes and word matching |
